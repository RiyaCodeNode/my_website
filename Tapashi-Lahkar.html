How I work

Technical foundations.
Product perspective.

My career spans software engineering, business analysis, Scrum leadership and product ownership. That combination helps me connect architectural realities with customer needs and commercial priorities.

I bring structure to ambiguous programmes: clarify the outcome, map dependencies, make trade-offs visible and align teams around an executable plan. At Expereo, that meant coordinating three to five engineering, operations and commercial teams at a time.

I’m particularly drawn to platform modernisation, API integrations, workflow automation and data transformation — work where good delivery depends on both technical judgement and shared direction.
